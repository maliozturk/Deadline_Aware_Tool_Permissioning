**Threat Intelligence Note: LockByte Ransomware-as-a-Service Campaign Targeting Healthcare Organizations**

**Date**: October 15, 2023

**Campaign Name**: LockByte v2.0

**Overview**:
LockByte, a well-known RaaS (Ransomware as a Service) campaign, has recently intensified its attacks on healthcare organizations in North America and Europe. This latest iteration of the malware includes advanced encryption methods and sophisticated affiliate models to maximize profitability.

**Encryption Methods**:
The LockByte v2.0 ransomware employs a combination of AES-256 for data encryption and RSA-4096 for key exchange, making it resistant to traditional decryption efforts. The malware also integrates with modern file systems to ensure that encrypted files cannot be recovered without the provided decryption keys.

**Encryption Process**:
1. **Initial Infection**: LockByte typically gains initial access through phishing emails containing malicious attachments or via compromised remote desktop protocols.
2. **Data Encryption**: Once inside, it scans for critical data (e.g., patient records, financial information) and encrypts these files in place.
3. **Key Exchange**: RSA-4096 keys are generated to secure the encryption process, ensuring that decryption without the ransom payment is virtually impossible.

**Affiliate Model**:
The LockByte v2.0 affiliate model has been restructured to enhance profitability and operational efficiency. Affiliates now receive detailed instructions on how to bypass antivirus software and leverage lateral movement techniques to spread the malware across networks. Each successful infection yields a commission based on encrypted data volume, with bonuses for large healthcare organizations.

**Negotiation Patterns**:
LockByte ransomware operators have introduced a dynamic pricing model where they evaluate the size of the organization and the criticality of the data before setting the ransom amount. Initial demands typically range from $300,000 to $500,000 USD, with higher amounts for hospitals and research institutions. They also offer a 24-hour deadline extension at an additional cost, encouraging organizations to pay quickly.

**Indicators of Compromise (IoCs)**:
- **Hostnames**: `lockbytecontrolpanel[.]com`, `securedataexchange[.]net`
- **IP Addresses**: 192.0.2.37, 203.0.113.45
- **File Names**: `EncryptedData.zip`, `RansomNote.txt`

**Detection and Mitigation Strategies**:
- Implement robust security measures such as multi-factor authentication (MFA), regular software updates, and employee training.
- Use advanced threat detection tools to monitor network traffic for unusual activity indicative of ransomware infections.
- Regularly back up data in a secure, isolated environment that is not connected to the main network.

This updated version of LockByte continues to pose a significant threat to healthcare organizations. Proactive defense strategies are crucial to mitigate risks and prevent potential breaches.
