### SOC Runbook Excerpt: Triage Workflow for Phishing Email Reported by an Employee

**Objective:** To efficiently triage a reported phishing email, analyze its components, isolate any potential threats, and communicate findings to the affected user.

---

#### **1. Initial Report Receipt**

Upon receiving a report of a suspected phishing email from an employee via [helpdesk ticketing system], immediately acknowledge receipt and initiate the following steps:

**Acknowledgment:** 
- Date: 2023-10-15
- Employee Name: Jane Doe
- Affected Device: PC1234

---

#### **2. Header Analysis**

**Objective:** To extract and analyze key information from the email header for further investigation.

**Steps:**
1. Retrieve the full email headers using the [email client’s] built-in feature or a third-party tool.
2. Identify and record the following:
   - Sender's email address
   - Received date
   - Email subject line
   - IP address of the sender (if available)
   - Hostnames from DNS records related to the sender domain

**Example:** 
- **Sender’s Email Address**: example@example.com
- **Received Date**: 2023-10-15T14:30:00Z
- **Subject Line**: Urgent: Update Your Account Information
- **IP Address**: 93.184.216.34 (reverse DNS resolves to mail.example.com)

**Analysis:** 
- Compare the sender's IP address against known malicious IPs in our threat intelligence database.
- Verify if any associated hostnames are flagged or suspicious.

---

#### **3. Attachment Sandboxing Procedure**

**Objective:** To safely analyze any attachments without exposing the SOC environment to potential threats.

**Steps:**
1. Isolate the email from the user’s inbox by moving it to a designated quarantine folder.
2. Use an on-demand sandboxing solution, such as [Cuckoo Sandbox], to analyze the attachment in a controlled environment.
3. Monitor for any suspicious activities, including network connections, file creation/deletion, and browser behavior.

**Sandbox Analysis:**
- Date of analysis: 2023-10-15T16:00:00Z
- Attachment Name: update_info.pdf
- Suspicious Activities Detected:
   - Network Connection to 93.184.216.34: Potential C&C Server
   - File Creation in Temp Directory
   - Browser Behavior Indicating Malware Download

**Conclusion:** 
- The attachment contains a potential malicious payload that attempts to establish a connection with an identified malicious IP address.

---

#### **4. User Communication Template**

**Objective:** To inform the affected user and provide guidance on next steps.

**Template:**
```
Subject: Phishing Email Report Follow-Up – Urgent Action Required

Dear Jane Doe,

Thank you for reporting the phishing email you received today. Our team has reviewed it, and we have taken the following actions:

- The sender’s email address (example@example.com) was verified against our threat intelligence database.
- We isolated the email and used [Cuckoo Sandbox] to analyze an attached file named "update_info.pdf".
- Our analysis revealed suspicious activities indicating potential malware download.

**Immediate Action Required:**
1. Do not open any emails or attachments from unknown sources.
2. Change your account password, especially if you entered personal information in the email.
3. Run a full system scan with your antivirus software to ensure no malicious files were downloaded.

If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact our IT support team at [helpdesk phone number] or [helpdesk email].

Thank you for your cooperation and attention to this matter.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
Security Operations Center
```
