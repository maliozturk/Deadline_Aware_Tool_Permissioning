**Threat Intelligence Note**

**Title:** Insider Threat Case Involving Data Exfiltration through Personal Cloud Storage

**Date:** March 15, 2024

**Reported by:** IT Security Team, XYZ Corporation

**Summary:**
XYZ Corporation has identified an insider threat incident involving data exfiltration through the use of personal cloud storage services. The incident was detected on February 27, 2024, following a routine data loss prevention (DLP) scan that flagged multiple large files being uploaded to a third-party cloud service.

**Behavioral Indicators:**
1. **Abnormal Data Uploads:** Large quantities of sensitive company documents were found in the personal cloud storage account of an employee named John Doe.
2. **Time of Day:** The majority of these uploads occurred between 03:00 and 04:00 AM, a time typically not aligned with normal work hours for most employees.
3. **File Types:** A significant number of documents included sensitive project reports, financial records, and internal communications.
4. **Frequency:** Over the course of one week (February 25-28), John Doe uploaded an average of 10 files per day, each ranging in size from 50MB to 3GB.

**DLP Bypass Methods:**
1. **Encryption:** The data was encrypted before upload to avoid detection by DLP tools.
2. **Time Manipulation:** Files were renamed and timestamps adjusted to match typical workday activity times, masking the actual exfiltration time.
3. **Use of Third-Party Services:** John Doe used a lesser-known cloud storage service that had less stringent security policies compared to company-approved services.

**Investigation Approach:**
1. **Initial Review:** Conducted an initial review of John Doe's access logs and personal device usage patterns, noting the unusual activity.
2. **Network Monitoring:** Deployed network monitoring tools to capture any traffic related to cloud storage uploads during irregular hours.
3. **Forensic Analysis:** Performed a forensic analysis on John Doe’s workstation, identifying executable files and scripts used for data manipulation.
4. **Interviews:** Conducted interviews with John Doe to understand his actions and motivations. He admitted to the exfiltration but claimed it was for a "personal project."
5. **Collaborative Investigation:** Engaged with cloud storage provider to review logs and identify any suspicious activities, which corroborated internal findings.

**Conclusion:**
The incident highlights the need for enhanced DLP measures that can detect encrypted data and account for time manipulation. It also underscores the importance of continuous monitoring and behavioral analytics in identifying insider threats. XYZ Corporation has since implemented stricter access controls and regular audits on all employee devices to prevent future incidents.

**Recommendations:**
- Increase monitoring of personal cloud storage usage.
- Implement more robust DLP solutions capable of detecting exfiltration through encryption.
- Conduct periodic security training focused on data protection and the risks associated with insider threats.
