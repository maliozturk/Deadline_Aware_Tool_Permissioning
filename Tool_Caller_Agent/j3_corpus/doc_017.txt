### SOC Runbook Excerpt: Handling a DDoS Attack Against Public-Facing Web Infrastructure

**Title:** DDoS Response Plan for Public-Facing Web Infrastructure

#### Introduction:
This document outlines the steps to be followed when responding to a Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attack targeting public-facing web infrastructure. The response plan includes traffic analysis, coordination with upstream Internet Service Providers (ISPs), and deployment of Web Application Firewall (WAF) rules.

---

**1. Initial Alert and Acknowledgment:**
- **Date:** February 20, 2023
- **Timestamp:** 14:05 UTC

**Alert Received from Security Monitoring System:** "High traffic volume detected on web server, exceeding baseline by 800%."

**Action:**
1. **Acknowledge the Alert:** Notify the incident response team and relevant stakeholders.
   - **Notification Email to Team:** "Attention all team members: We have detected a significant increase in traffic on our public-facing servers. Please refer to the DDoS response plan for immediate actions."
2. **Document Incident:** Record the time of detection, affected services, and initial observed symptoms.

---

**2. Traffic Analysis:**
- **Date:** February 20, 2023
- **Timestamp:** 14:15 UTC

**Action:**
1. **Capture Network Traffic:** Use tools like Wireshark or packet capture software to analyze the traffic patterns.
   - **Tools Used:** tcpdump for capturing and filtering network packets; Wireshark for analysis.
2. **Analyze Traffic Characteristics:** Identify if the attack is volumetric (aimed at overwhelming bandwidth), application layer (targeting specific applications), or a combination of both.
3. **Cross-Reference with Logs:** Review access logs, security event management system (SEMS) logs, and intrusion detection systems (IDS) for any suspicious activities.

---

**3. Coordination with Upstream ISP:**
- **Date:** February 20, 2023
- **Timestamp:** 14:30 UTC

**Action:**
1. **Contact ISP Support:** Inform the upstream Internet Service Provider about the ongoing DDoS attack.
   - **Email to ISP:** "Dear [ISP Support Team], we are experiencing a significant DDoS attack targeting our public-facing web servers at 14:05 UTC on February 20, 2023. Please apply QoS policies and rate limiting as soon as possible."
2. **Provide Details:** Share IP addresses of the attacking traffic, observed patterns, and any relevant log information.
   - **IP Addresses Provided:** 198.51.100.1-198.51.100.5
3. **Seek Assistance:** Request the ISP to perform deep packet inspection (DPI) or apply rate limiting on their end.

---

**4. Deployment of WAF Rules:**
- **Date:** February 20, 2023
- **Timestamp:** 15:00 UTC

**Action:**
1. **Identify Targeted Services:** Determine which web applications and services are being targeted by the DDoS attack.
2. **Configure WAF Rules:**
   - **Rule 1 (Volumetric Attack):** Enable rate limiting for HTTP requests to prevent overwhelming our servers.
     - **Action:** Configure the WAF to limit incoming connections from a single IP address within a 5-minute window to no more than 100 requests.
   - **Rule 2 (Application Layer Attack):** Implement challenge-response mechanisms and CAPTCHAs to filter out malicious traffic.
     - **Action:** Enable challenge-response mechanisms for suspicious HTTP requests, particularly those originating from known bad IP ranges or those with frequent failed login attempts.
