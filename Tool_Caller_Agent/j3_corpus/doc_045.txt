**Threat Intelligence Note**

**Title:** Analysis of Credential Harvesting Campaigns Utilizing Fake Microsoft 365 Login Pages

**Date:** October 20, 2023

**Executive Summary:**
Recent investigations have revealed a sophisticated credential harvesting campaign targeting users through the use of fake Microsoft 365 login pages. These phishing attempts are designed to mimic official Microsoft domains and employ advanced evasion techniques to bypass security measures.

**Infrastructure Analysis:**

The campaign operates primarily via a series of subdomains registered under the domain `fakemail365.com`. The primary landing page is hosted on `login.fakemail365.com`, which directs users to secondary pages that appear identical to Microsoft's official login portal. These pages are built using legitimate-looking HTML and CSS, making them highly convincing. A notable IP address associated with this campaign is 192.0.2.4 (for testing purposes; actual IPs may differ), used for hosting the malicious content.

**Evasion Techniques:**

The attackers have employed several techniques to evade detection:
- **Domain Spoofing:** Subdomains like `login.fakemail365.com` are used to mimic legitimate Microsoft URLs, reducing the likelihood of user suspicion.
- **Dynamic Content Generation:** The pages generate unique identifiers for each session to avoid signature-based detection methods. This makes traditional URL and content-based filters less effective.
- **SSL/TLS Certificates:** Malicious domains often obtain valid SSL/TLS certificates from Certificate Authorities (CAs) to establish a sense of legitimacy, further complicating the detection process.

**Detection Strategies:**

To combat this threat, organizations should implement the following detection strategies:
1. **URL Filtering and Phishing Awareness Training:** Educate employees on recognizing phishing attempts and ensure that URL filters are updated with known malicious domains.
2. **Behavioral Analysis Tools:** Deploy tools that monitor network traffic for unusual patterns, such as unexpected outbound connections or requests to suspicious IP addresses.
3. **Email Security Gateways:** Implement advanced email security gateways that can identify and block phishing emails containing links to fake login pages.
4. **Endpoint Protection Solutions:** Use endpoint protection solutions with up-to-date threat intelligence feeds to detect and mitigate malicious activities on user devices.

**Conclusion:**

This campaign demonstrates the evolving nature of phishing attacks, utilizing sophisticated techniques to harvest credentials. Organizations must remain vigilant and continuously update their security measures to protect against such threats. Regular training and awareness programs should be implemented to ensure that employees are prepared to identify and respond to these advanced threats.
