### Containment Procedure for Compromised Windows Endpoint Detected via EDR Alert

#### Objective:
To contain the compromised endpoint to prevent further compromise or data exfiltration while preserving evidence for forensic analysis.

---

**Step 1: Identification of Compromised Host**
- **Action:** Upon receiving an alert from the EDR (Endpoint Detection and Response) tool, immediately identify the affected host. Verify the hostname is `server02` with IP address `192.168.1.35`.
- **Timeline:** Immediate action within 5 minutes of detection.

**Step 2: Isolation of Compromised Host**
- **Action:** Ensure the compromised server, `server02`, is isolated from the network to prevent lateral movement.
    - Use network security tools (e.g., firewalls) to segment `server02` into a separate VLAN or subnet.
    - Disconnect any physical connections if necessary.
- **Timeline:** Within 15 minutes of isolation.

**Step 3: Evidence Preservation**
- **Action:** Collect and preserve evidence from the compromised host without altering it. Use read-only mechanisms where possible.
    - Enable write-protection on local storage devices.
    - Document all actions taken to prevent contamination of evidence.
- **Timeline:** Immediately upon isolation.

**Step 4: System Analysis and Logging Review**
- **Action:** Perform a comprehensive analysis of `server02` using the EDR tool. Focus on:
    - Reviewing logs for suspicious activities, including unauthorized access attempts or data exfiltration.
    - Checking for signs of malware infection such as unusual processes, registry modifications, or unexpected file activity.
- **Timeline:** Within 30 minutes of isolation.

**Step 5: Communication and Escalation**
- **Action:** Notify the incident response team via a secure communication channel. Provide details including:
    - Hostname (`server02`), IP address (`192.168.1.35`), and initial findings.
    - Request further instructions for containment, remediation, and forensics.
- **Timeline:** Within 5 minutes of completing the isolation and evidence preservation steps.

**Step 6: Escalation Criteria**
- **Criteria:** If the compromised host is part of a critical business system or contains sensitive data, escalate immediately to the CIRT (Computer Incident Response Team) for further action. Additionally, if multiple hosts are affected within a short period, this also requires immediate escalation.
- **Action:** Provide detailed logs and screenshots as evidence.

**Step 7: Documentation**
- **Action:** Document all steps taken in the incident response logbook. Record timestamps, actions performed, and any anomalies observed.
- **Timeline:** Continuously update throughout the containment phase.

---

By following these steps, the security operations center can effectively contain a compromised Windows endpoint while ensuring that evidence is preserved for subsequent analysis and remediation efforts.
