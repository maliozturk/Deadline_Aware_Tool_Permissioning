**Threat Intelligence Note: LockByte Ransomware-as-a-Service Campaign Targeting Healthcare Organizations**

**Date:** October 12, 2023

**Campaign Overview:**
LockByte is a well-known ransomware-as-a-service (RaaS) campaign that has recently intensified its targeting of healthcare organizations. This sophisticated cyber threat leverages a multi-step approach to maximize financial gains and operational disruptions for victims.

**Threat Actors:**
The LockByte RaaS platform operates under the leadership of a group known as "Silver Bullet," which has been active since 2019. The group offers a comprehensive service package that includes encryption tools, technical support, and a detailed affiliate model to streamline operations.

**Affiliate Model:**
LockByte's affiliate program is highly structured, offering various tiers based on the volume of victims compromised:
- **Tier 1:** $5,000 for up to 2 victim organizations
- **Tier 2:** $7,500 for 3-5 victim organizations
- **Tier 3:** $15,000 for more than 6 victim organizations

Affiliates are provided with a unique affiliate ID and access to the LockByte dashboard, which includes:
- Real-time tracking of compromised targets
- Automated encryption tools
- Support for negotiating ransom payment terms
- A dedicated support team for technical assistance

**Encryption Methods:**
LockByte employs advanced cryptographic techniques to ensure the effectiveness of its attacks. The primary encryption method used is AES-256 in CBC mode, which is known for its robustness and difficulty in decryption without the key. Additionally, the ransomware variant incorporates a file deletion routine that targets critical system files, rendering affected systems unusable until the ransom is paid.

**Negotiation Patterns:**
Ransom demands are typically negotiated through secure communication channels such as Telegram or encrypted messaging apps like Signal. Affiliates often leverage psychological tactics to pressure victims into compliance:
- **Initial Demand:** A starting ransom of $10,000 to $20,000 is usually proposed.
- **Extension Periods:** If no response is received within 48 hours, the demand may increase by 50%.
- **Payment Methods:** Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies are favored for transactions due to their anonymity.

**Recent Incidents:**
On October 1, 2023, LockByte affiliates targeted St. Mary's Hospital in Springfield, USA, compromising patient records and critical medical systems. The hospital was forced to shut down non-essential services as a precautionary measure while negotiating the ransom with Silver Bullet affiliates.

**Mitigation Strategies:**
Healthcare organizations are advised to implement robust cybersecurity measures, including:
- Regular data backups
- Patch management and software updates
- Employee training on phishing and social engineering tactics
- Incident response planning

By understanding the operational dynamics of LockByte RaaS, security professionals can better prepare for and mitigate the risks associated with such ransomware campaigns.
