### SOC Runbook Excerpt: Triage Workflow for a Phishing Email Reported by an Employee

#### Header Analysis Steps:

1. **Email Source Identification**:
   - Verify the sender's email address to determine if it matches known legitimate sources.
   - Check the domain name and ensure that it aligns with the expected business domain (e.g., `companyname.com` instead of `compayname.com`).
   - Cross-reference the sender’s email address with our internal DNS records.

2. **Email Content Inspection**:
   - Review the email for any suspicious elements such as grammatical errors, unusual links, or odd formatting.
   - Analyze the subject line and body content for signs of phishing attempts (e.g., urgent requests, threats, promises of rewards).

3. **Attachment Analysis**:
   - Identify if there are any attachments in the email.
   - Use a secure sandbox environment to open the attachment without executing its contents on our network.

4. **URL Inspection**:
   - If hyperlinks are present, hover over them (without clicking) to inspect the URL for authenticity and legitimacy.
   - Use online services like `urlvoid.com` or similar tools to scan URLs for known malicious activity.

#### Attachment Sandboxing Procedure:

1. **Isolate the Email Environment**:
   - Place the email in a quarantined environment isolated from our main network.
   
2. **Extract and Analyze Attachments**:
   - Use a dedicated sandbox (e.g., `Cuckoo Sandbox` or `Malwarebytes Email Scanner`) to extract and analyze the attachment.
   - Monitor for any suspicious activities such as process creation, file system changes, network connections, or registry modifications.

3. **Generate Report**:
   - Document findings from the sandbox analysis, including any malicious indicators detected (e.g., command-and-control server communications, payloads).
   - If necessary, conduct further investigations into these indicators.

4. **Containment and Remediation**:
   - Isolate affected systems if suspicious activity is detected.
   - Notify relevant teams for immediate remediation actions (e.g., patch management, endpoint detection).

#### User Communication Template:

---

**Subject: Urgent: Phishing Email Investigation**

Dear [Employee's Name],

We have received a report that you may have received a phishing email. Our Security Operations Center (SOC) team is conducting an investigation to ensure the security of our systems and data.

Please provide the following details for your assistance:
- Time and date when you received the email.
- Exact subject line and content of the email.
- Attachments, if any, that were opened or viewed.

We recommend taking the following steps until further notice:
1. **Do not click on any links** in the email unless verified through our IT support channels.
2. **Do not open any attachments** from unknown sources.
3. If you clicked a link or opened an attachment and encountered any unusual behavior, please immediately notify us.

Thank you for your cooperation in maintaining the security of our organization.

Best regards,

[Your Name]
Security Operations Center
[Company Name]
Phone: [Phone Number]
