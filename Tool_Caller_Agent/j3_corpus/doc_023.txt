The IP address 198.51.100.42 is associated with command-and-control (C2) traffic related to the Zeus.XL malware family. This IP was first observed on October 12, 2023, and has been used for establishing communication between infected hosts and the C2 server.

### Detailed Analysis
Zeus.XL is a variant of the Zeus banking trojan known for its sophisticated capabilities in stealing financial information from users' computers. The IP address 198.51.100.42 has been identified as a C2 server used by threat actors to issue commands and retrieve stolen data.

The WHOIS record for the domain c2.cybersecure.com, registered under CyberSecure Inc., is suspicious as it uses an innocuous name that could mislead potential victims. The registrant information does not provide any specific contact details, which is typical of many C2 infrastructure domains used by threat actors to hide their identities.
