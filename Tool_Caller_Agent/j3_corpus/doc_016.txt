### SOC Runbook Excerpt: Unauthorized Lateral Movement

**Title:** Investigating Unauthorized Lateral Movement Detected by Network Monitoring

**Objective:** To investigate an incident where unauthorized lateral movement has been detected within the network, ensuring the identification of affected systems, credential resets, and forensic evidence collection.

---

#### **1. Incident Overview**

- **Date/Time:** September 20, 2023, 14:35 UTC
- **Source:** Network Intrusion Detection System (NIDS)
- **Affected Systems:** 
  - Hostname: `HR-Workstation-1` (IP Address: 192.168.10.25)
  - Hostname: `Finance-Server-3` (IP Address: 192.168.11.50)

#### **2. Initial Response**

**Step 1:** Isolate the affected systems to prevent further unauthorized access.

   - Disconnect `HR-Workstation-1` and `Finance-Server-3` from the network.
   
**Step 2:** Notify the Incident Response Team (IRT) and relevant stakeholders, including legal counsel if necessary.

---

#### **3. Investigation**

**Step 1: Enumeration of Affected Hosts**

   - Use PowerShell scripts to list all processes running on `HR-Workstation-1` and `Finance-Server-3`.
   
     ```powershell
     Get-Process | Select-Object ProcessName, Id, StartTime
     ```

   - Check for any suspicious or non-standard services, network connections, or open ports.

**Step 2: Credential Reset Procedures**

   - For `HR-Workstation-1`:
     - Prompt the user to change their password immediately.
     - Use a secure method to perform the password reset through an administrative account.
     - Ensure the old credentials are deleted from all systems where they may have been cached.

   - For `Finance-Server-3`:
     - Reset the administrator account and any other high-privilege accounts that were potentially compromised.
     - Implement multi-factor authentication (MFA) for these accounts moving forward.

**Step 3: Forensic Image Collection**

   - Use a dedicated forensic tool to create an image of `HR-Workstation-1`'s hard drive, preserving the current state of the system.
   
     ```shell
     dd if=/dev/sda of=hr_workstation_20230920.img bs=4M status=progress
     ```

   - Similarly, collect a forensic image of `Finance-Server-3`'s hard drive.

     ```shell
     dd if=/dev/sdb of=finance_server_20230920.img bs=4M status=progress
     ```

   - Document the time and method used for collection to ensure chain-of-custody is maintained.

---

#### **4. Reporting**

**Step 1:** Compile a detailed report including:
- Timeline of events.
- Description of actions taken.
- Identification of affected systems.
- Recommendations for improving security posture, such as implementing more robust access controls or enhancing monitoring tools.

**Step 2:** Share the findings with relevant stakeholders and maintain communication throughout the incident response process to ensure transparency and accountability.

---

This runbook excerpt provides a structured approach to addressing unauthorized lateral movement within an organization's network, ensuring that affected systems are isolated, credentials are securely reset, and forensic evidence is properly collected for analysis.
