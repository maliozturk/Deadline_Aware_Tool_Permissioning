5. SYNTHESIZE: Write a final analytical answer that integrates all
   retrieved evidence into a coherent assessment.

Rules:
- You MUST call the retrieve tool at least twice (and up to three times).
- Each retrieval call should use a DIFFERENT query targeting a different
  angle of the investigation.
- Hard limit: 1500 output tokens maximum.
- Use professional threat-intelligence language.
- Structure your response with clear sections: Investigation Steps,
